# Non-production safety disclaimer

ScenarioForge 0.1.0 is research and evaluation software. It is not certified
for automotive, robotics, medical, industrial, or other safety-critical use.
Simulation results do not establish real-world safety, regulatory compliance,
fitness for deployment, or absence of hazards.

Do not connect ScenarioForge to vehicle controls. Do not use its output as the
sole basis for a release, operational, or safety decision. Qualified humans
must independently review scenarios, simulator assumptions, evidence integrity,
and any real-world validation plan. MetaDrive behavior, numerical tolerances,
capacity measurements, and exact replay apply only to the pinned versions and
profiles identified by the evidence index.
